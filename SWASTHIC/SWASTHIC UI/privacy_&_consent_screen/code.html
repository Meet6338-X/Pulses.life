SWASTHIC (SAMVED-VAKR) uses anonymized health data to protect our city. Learn how we handle your information ethically.

We only collect anonymized symptom data and blurred location info. Your personal identity, such as your full name or exact GPS coordinates, is never linked to the public health reports.

The data is aggregated to create city-wide health trends. This helps government bodies deploy emergency response teams and medical supplies to neighborhoods that need them most in real-time.
